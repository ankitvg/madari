---
name: evidence-check
description: Check whether a brief's conclusions are supported by its source material.
---

Before finalizing a brief, compare each conclusion with the supplied or retrieved
source material. Flag missing denominators, unclear time windows, selection
bias, and causal claims unsupported by the evidence. Return a short evidence
check identifying supported conclusions and remaining uncertainties. Do not
invent additional observations or treat a successful check as a guarantee.
